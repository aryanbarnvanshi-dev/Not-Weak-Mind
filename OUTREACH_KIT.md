## 🌐 2. Reddit / Forum Post Draft

**Target Subreddits:** `r/india`, `r/Indian_Academia`, `r/adhdindia`, `r/dyslexia`, `r/opensource`

**Post Title:**  
> *I built "Not-Weak-Mind" — a 100% free, private, bilingual (Hindi/English) screening tool for children often labeled "kamzor dimaag" due to undetected Dyslexia/ADHD. Looking for feedback from educators and parents.*

**Post Body:**
```markdown
Hi everyone,

Growing up in India, we've all seen children who struggle with reading, math, or sitting still in class. In most schools and homes, the automatic response is: *"Iska dimaag kamzor hai"* or *"Yeh to bas badmaash aur aalsi hai"*. 

The reality? Over 35 million children in India live with Specific Learning Difficulties (Dyslexia, Dyscalculia, Dysgraphia, ADHD), but formal neuropsychological assessments cost ₹5,000–₹25,000 in private clinics. 90% of schools have zero access to counselors.

To help bridge this gap, I built **Not-Weak-Mind (EduScreen)** — an ethical, open-source educational screening tool and cognitive visualizer.

### Key Features:
1. **100% Client-Side & Private:** Zero backend, zero telemetry, zero database. Child names and answers never leave the device.
2. **Bilingual (English & हिन्दी):** Instant 1-click localization so non-English speaking parents can use it.
3. **Web Speech Audio Narration:** Questions can be read aloud in natural speech (`en-US` and `hi-IN`) so dyslexic students aren't forced to struggle through 80 text prompts.
4. **Multi-Domain Radar & Bar Visualizer:** Breaks down cognitive profiles across 15 domains (Working Memory, Processing Speed, Fine Motor, Anxiety, etc.) instead of a generic pass/fail score.
5. **Multi-Tiered Action Plans:** Gives practical, concrete steps for Classrooms, Home, Assistive Tech, and formal clinical referral guidance.
6. **Zero Cost / Offline PWA:** Caches offline and runs even on budget phones with spotty 2G/3G connectivity. We also build an offline Android APK.

🔗 **Live App (Works on any browser):** https://aryanbarnvanshi-dev.github.io/Not-Weak-Mind/  
💻 **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/aryanbarnvanshi-dev/Not-Weak-Mind  

I would love honest feedback from teachers, psychologists, parents, and neurodivergent individuals:
* Are the question phrasing and Hindi translations natural?
* Does the 15-domain breakdown provide genuine clarity?
* What additional accommodations or features would help in Indian classroom settings?

Thank you for your time and guidance!
```

## 🏫 4. How to Conduct a 5-Minute School Demonstration

If you are speaking to a local school teacher or principal:
1. **Open the site on your phone:** `https://aryanbarnvanshi-dev.github.io/Not-Weak-Mind/`
2. **Demonstrate Speed:** Click **"📊 Preview Sample Profile"** — in 1 second, show the colorful **15-Domain Bar Chart** and the **4-Quadrant Cognitive Radar**.
3. **Show Teacher Mode:** Switch back, show how a teacher can select *"Teacher / Parent Observation Mode"* and *"⚡ Rapid Screener (15 Questions)"*.
4. **Show Accessibility:** Click **`[ 🌐 हिन्दी ]`** and tap **`🔊 Read Aloud`** to show that it speaks Hindi and English aloud.
5. **Show Export:** Tap **"🖨️ Print / Save PDF"** to demonstrate that teachers can print out the report to hand directly to parents during Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTM).

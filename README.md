## 🤔 What is This?

**Not-Weak-Mind (EduScreen)** is a simple questionnaire created by a high school student to help people think about different learning styles and potential learning challenges.

### Simple Purpose
The tool asks **80 questions** to explore:
- Reading & spelling challenges
- Math difficulty
- Writing challenges
- Attention & focus
- Organization & planning
- Processing speed
- Memory issues
- Anxiety about learning
- Motivation
- And more...

### What Happens After
You get a simple report showing:
- Which areas might have challenges
- Which areas seem fine
- Some basic suggestions
- **A strong recommendation to talk to a professional**

12. **Sensitivity to Sights/Sounds** - Sensory sensitivity
13. **Hand & Fine Motor Skills** - Writing & coordination
14. **Understanding People** - Social learning
15. **Talking & Expressing** - Communication

## 👥 Assessment Modes

### Mode 1: Student Answers
**Who:** The student themselves
**Questions:** "I mix up letters..." "I zone out..."
**Good for:** Student awareness, self-reflection
**Limitations:** Students might not be accurate judges

### Mode 2: Parent/Teacher Observes
**Who:** Parent or teacher watches the student
**Questions:** "Student confuses letters..." "Often daydreams..."
**Good for:** Outside perspective
**Limitations:** Observer might miss things or have bias

**Best practice:** Do BOTH and compare results!

### The Report Shows:
1. **Your answers** - What you said for each area
2. **A simple chart** - Visual of the results

## 📖 The "Not-Weak-Mind" Mission

In many households and classrooms across India and worldwide, millions of neurodivergent children are punished, beaten, or labeled as **"kamzor dimaag" (weak-minded)**, **"lazy"**, or **"deliberately misbehaving"** because their struggles are invisible:
* A child with **Dyslexia** sees letter inversions (like mixing 'b' and 'd') and feels physically exhausted reading text.
* A child with **ADHD / Executive Dysfunction** genuinely forgets the second step of an instruction because their working memory is overloaded.
* A child with **Dysgraphia** experiences severe hand cramping holding a pencil.

Formal neuropsychological assessments often cost **₹5,000 to ₹25,000**, creating an insurmountable barrier for 95% of families.

**EduScreen acts as a "Cognitive Thermometer":**
Just as a thermometer doesn't cure an illness but alerts parents that a child has a fever rather than making excuses, EduScreen objectively visualizes cognitive balance across 15 domains, turning a punishing conflict (*"Why are you so lazy?"*) into constructive support (*"Let's try a visual checklist and audio tools"*).

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🎮 100% Pure Game-Based Cognitive Assessment Lab (v2.0.0)
Skip text-heavy questionnaires entirely! EduScreen includes an interactive **3-Minute Cognitive Game Circuit** providing objective, gamified assessment across 4 core mini-games:
- **🔤 Letter Mirror Detective:** 16-tile glyph discrimination identifying mirror reversals (`b/d/p/q`) for orthographic dyslexia screening.
- **🎯 Go / No-Go Sustained Focus Drill:** 75/25 GO/WAIT stimulus testing millisecond response latency, commission errors (false alarms), and inhibitory vigilance (ADHD).
- **🔢 Memory Echo Sequence:** Audio-visual musical pad sequence tracking multi-component working memory span.
- **🎲 Rapid Number Dash:** Dynamic SVG Weber-ratio dot subitizing testing Approximate Number System (ANS) acuity for dyscalculia detection.

### 2. ⏱️ Objective Behavioral Telemetry & 5-Pillar Synthesis
Replaces subjective self-reports with real-time empirical measurements (reaction latency, false alarms, memory spans, subitizing accuracy). Derives mathematical percentiles for the **5 Cognitive Pillars**: *Attention, Visual-Spatial, Working Memory, Numerical ANS, and Processing Speed*.

### 3. 📜 Statutory Accommodations & CBSE Examination Provisions
Automatically maps identified cognitive flags to official government accommodations under **CBSE Examination Guidelines** and the **RPwD Act 2016**:
- **CBSE Rule 24.1:** Basic Mathematics track (Code 241) and simple calculator allowance for dyscalculia profiles.
- **CBSE Compensatory Time:** 20 minutes compensatory extra time per hour and exemption from spelling deductions for dyslexia profiles.
- **Classroom Accommodations:** Supervised movement breaks and teacher-proximity seating for executive function profiles.

### 4. 🌐 Bilingual by Design (English & हिन्दी)
Instant 1-click toggle between English and Devanagari Hindi. Translates all game interfaces, 80 questions, 15 domain explanations, risk badges, telemetry tables, and CBSE recommendations.

### 5. 🔊 Integrated Audio Narration (Web Speech API)
Testing a dyslexic child with dense blocks of text is a clinical paradox. EduScreen includes a built-in **`🔊 Read Aloud`** button that reads questions out loud in natural English (`en-US`) or Hindi (`hi-IN`) with measured pacing.

### 3. 📊 Dual Multidimensional Visualizations
* **15-Domain Score Profile:** High-DPI bar visualizer with color-coded risk stratification (Green: Strength/Low, Blue: Mild, Amber: Moderate, Red: Strong).
* **4-Quadrant Cognitive Radar (Spider) Chart:** Plots balance across:
  1. *Academic & Language* (Dyslexia, Dyscalculia, Dysgraphia, Communication)
  2. *Executive & Attention* (Focus, Working Memory, Processing Speed, Planning)
  3. *Affective & Behavioral* (Learning Anxiety, Test Anxiety, Motivation, Social)
  4. *Sensorimotor* (Sensory Sensitivity, Fine Motor Coordination, Recall)

### 4. 🏫 Tiered MTSS Action Plans
Provides actionable, practical interventions across 4 levels:
* 🏫 **Classroom Accommodations:** Untimed tests, visual checklists, slant boards, oral response alternatives.
* 🏠 **Home Exercises:** Bilateral crafting, 4-7-8 calming routines, backpack landing pads.
* 💻 **Assistive Technology:** Phonics apps, Speech-to-Text tools, blue-light filters.
* 🩺 **Clinical Guidance:** Explicit recommendations for Educational Psychologists, Speech-Language Pathologists (SLP), or Occupational Therapists (OT).

### 5. 🛡️ 100% Client-Side Privacy Architecture
* **Zero Cloud Storage:** Student names and test results exist solely in volatile device memory and local browser cache.
* **Zero Telemetry or Cookies:** No third-party trackers, analytics, or behavioral cookies.
* **Offline PWA:** Caches locally via Service Worker `sw.js` for full offline functionality.

### 6. 📁 Clinical Multi-Format Exports
* 🖨️ **Print / PDF Export:** Tailored `@media print` layout formatting A4 clinical summaries with clean margins and risk badges.
* 📥 **JSON Psychometric Payload:** Download raw assessment state for educational psychologists or archival records.
* 📊 **CSV Export:** Tabular comma-separated values for school records or multi-student comparison.

## ⚠️ Important Educational Disclaimer & Legal Safe Harbor

> **EduScreen is an educational awareness screening survey, NOT a formal medical, psychiatric, or clinical diagnostic evaluation.**

* **What it is:** A structured observational survey designed to identify cognitive learning patterns, stimulate constructive discussions between parents and educators, and guide supportive classroom accommodations (Tier 1 MTSS).
* **What it is NOT:** A clinical diagnosis or psychological certification. It does not replace comprehensive evaluation by licensed clinicians (such as Clinical Psychologists, Developmental Pediatricians, or Certified Special Educators).
* **Statutory Immunity:** In full accordance with the **National Medical Commission (NMC) Act 2019**, the **Mental Healthcare Act 2017**, and the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023**, EduScreen does not constitute medical practice, forms no doctor-patient relationship, and collects zero personal data on remote servers.
* **Fair Dealing & Fair Use:** Academic construct citations are made under **Section 52(1)(a) of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957** and **Section 107 of the US Copyright Act** for educational research and review. All 80 behavioral prompts are original works independently authored in vernacular language by Aryan Kumar with zero verbatim derivation from commercial psychometric batteries.

## 🔬 Scientific Grounding across 15 Cognitive Domains

All 15 domains assessed by EduScreen map directly to established, public-domain cognitive science constructs and peer-reviewed educational literature:
* **Dyslexia:** Phonological Deficit Model (*International Dyslexia Association*, Shaywitz, 2003; Lyon et al., 2003)
* **Dyscalculia:** Numerical Cognition & Approximate Number System (*Butterworth*, 2010; *Geary*, 2004)
* **Dysgraphia:** Simple View of Writing & Graphomotor Integration (*Berninger & Amtmann*, 2009; *Graham*, 1996)
* **Attention:** Behavioral Inhibition & Sustained Vigilance (*Barkley*, 1997; *DSM-5 Observational Criteria*)
* **Executive Function:** Goal-Directed Cognitive Control (*Diamond*, 2013; *Baddeley*, 1996)
* **Processing Speed:** Cattell-Horn-Carroll Gs Factor (*Kail*, 1991; *McGrew*, 2005)
* **Working Memory:** Multi-Component Model (*Baddeley & Hitch*, 1974; *Alloway*, 2009)
* **Recall & Retrieval:** Long-Term Retrieval Glr & Episodic Memory (*Tulving*, 1972)
* **Learning & Test Anxiety:** Cognitive Interference & Evaluative Threat (*Cassady & Johnson*, 2002; *Spielberger*, 1983)
* **Academic Motivation:** Self-Determination Theory & Mindset (*Deci & Ryan*, 2000; *Dweck*, 2006)
* **Sensory Sensitivity:** Dunn's Sensory Processing Framework (*Dunn*, 1997)
* **Fine Motor Skills:** Visual-Motor Integration Framework (*Beery VMI Framework*, 2010)
* **Social Learning & Communication:** Theory of Mind & Pragmatic Language (*Baron-Cohen et al.*, 1985; *Bishop*, 2006)

## 🛠️ Technology Stack & Engineering Standards

* **Zero-Dependency Architecture:** Written in vanilla HTML5, CSS3 Custom Properties, and modular ES6 JavaScript. No heavy frameworks, no external runtime dependencies.
* **High-DPI Canvas Rendering:** Double-buffered Canvas 2D engine with devicePixelRatio auto-scaling and fallback bounding.
* **Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AAA Compliant):**
  * Atkinson Hyperlegible accessible typography.
  * Reading ruler tracking guide.
  * Contrast ratios exceeding 7:1 across all risk badges and disclaimers.
  * High-visibility `:focus-visible` indicators and WAI-ARIA `radiogroup`/`radio` attributes.
* **Automated CI Test Suite:** 55 comprehensive integration tests running on native Node.js 24 test runner (`node --test tests/tier1-features/*.test.js`) executed automatically on every push via GitHub Actions in ~1.28 seconds.
